Settings

Hyper realistic: higher resolution and shadows, film look, reverb, no crosshair or health bar, heavier recoil and sway, deadlier hits, faster bleeding, limping from leg wounds, scope breathing, more gore. Shadow quality updates when you start a game.

HOW TO PLAY — ALL CONTROLS

MOVEMENT

WASD Move

Shift Sprint (lowers your weapon)

Space Jump (hard landings shake the camera)

Mouse Look around

Esc Pause / menu

COMBAT

LMB Fire / slash / run the chainsaw

RMB Aim down sights

R Reload: drop the mag, grab a fresh one, seat it, then cock the weapon

F Cock back / rack the weapon

1–8 Pistol · Chainsaw · Knife · Rifle · Sniper · M4-Carbine · AMW · SMG

ATTACHMENTS

Z Silencer (quiet, citizens hear less)

X Laser sight

C Extended magazine

V Cycle scope 1x – 4x (guns only)

SURVIVAL

B Bandage (stops bleeding, heals a little)

Tab Anatomy view: per-limb damage and bleeding

K Show / hide the key bar

Bandages refill between Arena waves.

SANDBOX

9 Build mode — LMB place, RMB remove, Y change material

G Spawn a citizen · H spawn an enemy · J spawn a crowd (at your crosshair)

1–8 Leave build mode and pick a weapon

MAP CREATOR

Play → Map Creator. Build mode: free-fly camera.

WASD Fly · Space Up · C Down · Shift Fast

LMB Place block · RMB Remove block or NPC · Wheel / Y Material

G H J Place citizens / enemy / crowd · E Set player spawn

T Test mode: play your map, press T again to return to building (everything resets).

Esc menu: Save, Load, Export / Import text, Clear. Saved maps can be played in Sandbox → Custom.

TIPS

Citizens panic at gunshots. Kill an innocent in front of witnesses and word spreads (WANTED).

Stronger weapons do more damage to buildings.
